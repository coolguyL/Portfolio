About Me

Visionary Leader in Education and Business

Mr. Sujit Dash is a dynamic and forward-thinking educationalist and business leader with a passion for creating impactful institutions that serve communities across Odisha and beyond. With a clear vision to provide quality education and essential services, he has established multiple successful ventures spanning education, real estate, hospitality, and logistics.

As the Chairman of Gosain International School, he oversees multiple branches in strategic locations including Jajpur Town, Chandini BJB Nagar -Bhubaneswar, and Trisulia-Cuttack, ensuring that world-class education reaches every corner of the region.

His business philosophy is centered around sustainable growth, community development, and creating opportunities for the next generation of leaders. Through his diverse portfolio of companies, Mr. Dash has created hundreds of jobs and contributed significantly to the local economy.

Personal Profile

Journey of a Visionary Educationalist & Business Leader

Early Life & Background

Mr. Sujit Dash was born on 3rd May 1985 in Bhodar, Nimapada, Puri District, Odisha, India, into a humble and value-driven Baishnab joint family. Raised in an environment that emphasized education, honesty, discipline, and service to society.

His father, Late Subodha Chandra Dash, and his mother, Smt. Pramila Kumari Dash, played a vital role in shaping his moral foundation. Growing up alongside two brothers and five sisters, Mr. Dash developed a strong sense of responsibility, cooperation, and leadership from an early age.

Family: Married to Smt. Banishree Dash (Director of Gosain Developers Pvt. Ltd.), father to Brijnandan Dash. His family's unwavering support has been a pillar in his journey.

Academic Journey & Early Struggles

Completed schooling in Nimapada and passed Class-X examination in second division. Compelled by circumstances, he temporarily paused formal education, which later became a defining catalyst in his life.

With an initial capital of just Rs.25,000, he established a Poultry farming enterprise while simultaneously founding Gosain Coaching Centre in 1999 in his village. During this period, he also worked as an electrical wiring technician.

Encouraged by early entrepreneurial success, he resumed formal education, completing +2 in Commerce from Nimapada College, followed by Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) in 2004. Parallel to his studies, he expanded his ventures under the brands Gosain Coaching and Gosain Electrical Wiring Agency.

Corporate Exposure & National-Level Experience

Joined Dabur India Limited (2005–2006) serving in KBK districts of Odisha. Between 2007 and 2020, served with Shivshakti Group of Companies across multiple locations in India including Lucknow, Gorakhpur, Allahabad, Jaipur, Udaipur, Gurgaon, Indore, Bhopal, and Kota.

Culminated in a senior role with a monthly salary of Rs.72,000, reflecting professional growth and leadership capabilities.

Concurrently worked as an independent freelancer accounting professional (National & international level), leveraging financial software expertise and generating additional monthly income of up to Rs.1,00,000.

Turning Point: Return to Roots & Renewed Purpose

The demise of his father on 6 March 2020, followed by the COVID-19 pandemic, marked a profound turning point. He returned permanently to his hometown to fulfil familial responsibilities and manage ancestral properties.

This period of introspection deepened his resolve to build sustainable institutions that serve society, particularly in the field of education.

Leadership in Education & Institutional Development

Joined St. Xavier's Group of Schools as Chief Financial Officer (CFO) in late 2020. Played a pivotal role in shaping institutional excellence through:

  • Strategic financial planning and Management
  • CBSE compliance, audits, and regulatory frameworks
  • Administrative restructuring and operational efficiency
  • Budget optimization and HR systems
  • Infrastructure planning and school expansion initiatives

Gained deep insight into educational leadership, quality assurance, and academic administration over 15+ years of cumulative experience.
